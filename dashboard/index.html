Pipeline

STEP 1 · PYTHONValidate12 automated data quality checks on 105,996 raw reports
STEP 2 · NLPClassifyRegex rules map free-text narratives to 7 hazard categories
STEP 3 · SQLModelDuckDB star schema: fact_incident + 5 dimensions, integrity tested
STEP 4 · BIActInteractive dashboard and CAPA priorities for plant leadership
STEP 5 · PLMSpecifyRecipe BOM, allergen roll-up, where-used and spec review queue for 2,724 products
Limitations: OSHA SIR covers federal-OSHA jurisdictions, so state-plan states such as California and Washington are largely absent and figures are not a national total. "Inspection opened" is OSHA's regulatory follow-up, used as a proxy for an investigation trigger. Hazard categories are rule-based (first matching rule wins).
